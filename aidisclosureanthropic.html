What AI does in this product

Curioxy AI uses Claude, built by Anthropic, to identify items from photos. That's the entire scope of AI in this product — you point your camera at something, and Claude tells us what it is.

Everything after identification is real data, not AI. Marketplace listings, prices, and condition information come directly from live marketplace APIs — never generated, estimated, or predicted by AI, under any circumstance.

What's AI-made vs. human-made

AI-powered: the item-identification step only.

Human-made: the app's design and product decisions, all campaign materials (video, copy, imagery), and all marketplace search and display logic. No campaign imagery or video content is AI-generated.

Training data, consent, and credit

We integrate Claude via Anthropic's existing API — we are not training our own AI model from scratch, and we do not collect or curate our own training dataset. Questions about how the underlying model was trained, and Anthropic's approach to consent and credit for that training data, are governed entirely by Anthropic's own policies, not by us. Why this matters to us

Curioxy AI exists because AI-generated guesses about what something's worth aren't trustworthy — that's the whole premise of the product. We hold ourselves to the same standard with AI transparency generally: say exactly what the AI does, say exactly what it doesn't do, and don't blur the two.
